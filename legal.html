2. Design-First Order Process

  1. Submit a design: Send your own artwork, reference image or a clear design brief, together with the garment type, sizes, quantities and contact details.
  2. Design review: Our team reviews the material and may ask questions or request a clearer file or additional information.
  3. Proof and quotation: We confirm the proposed design, garment details, size breakdown, quantity, total price in RM and estimated delivery timeframe.
  4. Customer approval: The customer checks and approves the final proof and order summary in writing.
  5. Order acceptance: The order is accepted only when IT WORLD JERSEY confirms acceptance and provides any agreed payment or deposit instructions.

Incomplete, unclear, technically unsuitable or potentially infringing designs may be paused or declined. Do not make payment until the store has confirmed the order details and payment instructions through an official channel.
